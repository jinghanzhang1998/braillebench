# BrailleBench 0.1.0

Released: 2026-08-24

BrailleBench 0.1.0 is the first public, directly usable release of the benchmark.

## Included

- Five reasoning and question-answering datasets with 22,551 logical records.
- UEB Grade 1 and Grade 2 translations for every record.
- Braille ASCII, Unicode Braille, and dot-number representations.
- Seven core evaluation configurations plus full-Braille and cheat-sheet ablations.
- Provider-neutral inference integration, incremental checkpoints, and resume support.
- Deterministic data, checksum, scoring, and Braille-output validation tools.
